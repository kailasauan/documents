The documentary record of the United Ancient Nations

Treaties concluded with nations and communities, reports and expert opinions, judicial and administrative records, and the historical archives of the ancient nations — transcribed in full and published openly, so that the record can be read and cited by anyone.

Notices

Every record is published as readable text on its own page, not as a download-only file, so that it can be found by search engines and read on any device.

Certified copies of any record may be requested from the Secretariat.

Corrections to a transcription are welcome and are recorded against the record.
