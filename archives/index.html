Translation of the Coronation Deed from Mata Vibhutananda Puri for Arunachala SarvajnapeethamUAN-ARCHIVE-Religious-CoronationDeed-19940524-1
Deed · 24 May 1994 · Arunachala Sarvajnapeetham; Mata Vibhutananda Puri · 131 words

A translated coronation deed issued by Mata Vibhutananda Puri (Vibhutai/Kuppammal), a Brahma Yogini, documenting the initiation and coronation of Rajashekaran as her disciple with the new spiritual name Brahmasukhi. The deed dates to 24 May 1994 and was issued in Tiruvannamalai.

Significance of Adishaiva Lineage for Installation and Consecration: Selections from the Kāmikāgama Uttara Pada and Pauskara AgamaUAN-ARCHIVE-Religious-AdishaivaInitiation-UNDATED-1
Archive · Undated · Not stated in the document · 977 words

A compilation of Sanskrit verses with English translations from Hindu liturgical and philosophical texts, specifically the Kāmikāgama Uttara Pada and Pauskara Agama. The document presents teachings on the role and qualifications of Adishaiva priests in temple installation, consecration, and initiation rituals; the importance of guru-led initiation into the four spiritual paths (Charyapada, Kriyapada, Jnanapada, and Yogapada); and the prescribed ritual competencies of different social varnas and mendicants. The material is arranged thematically with Sanskrit source verses and explanatory English paraphrases.
