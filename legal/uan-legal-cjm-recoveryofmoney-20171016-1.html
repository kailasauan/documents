Judgment in Original Suit No. 367/2016: Nithyananda Foundation and Life Bliss Foundation v. Sri. Vinay Bharadwaj for Recovery of Money

This is a judgment issued by the Court of the Principal Senior Civil Judge and Chief Judicial Magistrate at Mysuru on 16 October 2017 in an original suit filed by Nithyananda Foundation and Life Bliss Foundation against Sri. Vinay Bharadwaj for recovery of money. The suit concerns a claim to recover a sum of Rs. 2,74,94,447.50 with interest, based on attorney's fees awards from prior proceedings in the Superior Court of the State of California. The defendant did not appear to contest the matter.
