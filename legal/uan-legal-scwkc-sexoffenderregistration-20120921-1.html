UAN-LEGAL-SCWKC-SexOffenderRegistration-20120921-1

Judgment and Sentence, Non-Felony, State of Washington v. Vinay Keshavan Bharadwaj

A judgment and sentencing order issued by the Superior Court of Washington for King County on the conviction of Vinay Keshavan Bharadwaj following trial on 08/14/2012. The defendant was found guilty of Count IV: Communication with a Minor for Immoral Purposes under RCW 9.68A.090(1). The order imposes conditions including imprisonment, probation supervision, DNA collection, sex offender registration, and prohibition on unsupervised contact with minors.
