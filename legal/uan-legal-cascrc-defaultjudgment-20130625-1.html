Judgment by Default in Life Bliss Foundation v. Aarthi Rao et al., Case No. RIC1105004

A judgment by default issued by the Superior Court of California, County of Riverside, in a civil case brought by Life Bliss Foundation, a California non-profit corporation, against defendant Aarthi Rao and others. The defendant failed to answer the complaint or appear within the time allowed by law, and the clerk entered a default judgment upon the plaintiff's application.
