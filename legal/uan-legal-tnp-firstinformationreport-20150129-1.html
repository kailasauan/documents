First Information Report (FIR) - Tamil Nadu Police

A First Information Report filed under Section 154 of the Code of Criminal Procedure by the Tamil Nadu Police. The document is a bilingual form (Tamil and English) used to record the initial information received at a police station concerning an alleged crime. It contains sections for recording complainant details, accused particulars, place and time of occurrence, property involved, and the narrative of the alleged offense.
