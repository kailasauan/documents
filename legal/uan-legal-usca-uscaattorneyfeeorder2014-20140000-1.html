opposition, points and authorities, and declarations filed by the 7 parties, and having heard argument of counsel, the Court finds that 1 On August 10, 2009, Plaintiff Vnay Bharadwaj and Gopal Reddy Sheelum, on behalf 9 of Defendants, entered into a valid, enforceable mutual release agreement, which was 10 even notarized (the "Release Agreement") The Release Agreement contained a release 11 provision at page 2, paragraph 3, which released any and all claims Plaintiff had as 12 against Defendants The Release Agreement also contained an attorney's fee provision 13 at page 6, paragraph 12, which provided, in relevant part "If any party to this 14 Agreement employs attorneys to enforce any rights arising out of or in relation to this 15 Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees " 16 2 On January 3, 2011, Mr Bharadwaj initiated this action, alleging both contract and tort 17 claims The tort claims were subject to the release provision of the Release Agreement, 18 and in an attempt to get around that release, the complaint alleged a claim for 19 declaratory relef that the Release Agreement between the parties was "null, void, and 20 Is 1 2 7 Defendants are the prevailing party in this matter as having successfully defended the 3 appeal and obtained a simple, unqualified dismissal of the appeal 4 8 As the prevailing party, Defendants are entitled to their reasonable attorney's fees on 5 appeal pursuant to the attorney's fee provision of the Release Agreement under Civil 6 Code § 1717 A judgment of dismissal of the action "on a contract" was already 7 rendered in Defendants' favor By his appeal, Plaintiff attempted - and failed -- to have the dismissal reversed so that he could pursue his action to invalidate the Release Agreement and assert the contractually-released tort claims against Defendants Thus, 10 as was the underlying action, the appeal of its dismissal was "on a contract" within the 11 meaning of Civil Code § 1717 By their opposition, Defendants fought to affirm the 12 dismissal so that the Release Agreement would remain enforceable and achieved that 13 result when the appellate court dismissed the appeal and awarded them their costs 14 Thus, Defendants obtained a simple, unqualified win on appeal and are therefore 15 entitled to their attorney's fees, as a matter of right 16 9 Moreover, the subject attorney's fee provision is broadly-worded and contemplates that 17 any attorney's fees incurred as relating to the Release Agreement warrant 18 reimbursement to the prevailing party There is no limitation within the fee provision to 19 exclude fees incurred on appeals The provision also does not condition the manner in 20 which a prevailing party prevails on the action in order to be deemed a prevailing party 21 As such, the provision entitles Defendants to its attorney's fees mcurred on appeal 22 10 The amount of attorney's fees requested by Defendants is full, fair, and reasonable 23 a Hourly Rates Defendants have presented substantial and sufficient evidence 24 that their attorneys' hourly rates on appeal of $400 for attorney Aviv L Tuchman, $350 25 for attorneys Michael C Dicecca and Andrew C Jhun, and $150 for law clerk Anthony 26 Gentile are reasonable and in line, if not more competitive, than the rates charged by 27 attorneys of comparable skill, reputation, and experience for similar cases On the other 28 hand, no evidence has been presented by Plaintiff challenging the moving party's 3 ORDER GRANTING DEFENDANTS' MOTION FOR ATTORNEY'S FEES
