INTERNATIONAL CRIMINAL POLICE ORGANIZATION ORGANISATION INTERNATIONALE DE POLICE CRIMINELLE ORGANIZACIÓN INTERNACIONAL DE POLICÍA CRIMINAL المنظمة الدولية للشرطة الجنائية INTERPOL

TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN Ref: LA/88732-1587

The General Secretariat of the International Criminal Police Organization-INTERPOL hereby certifies that, as of today, Mr SWAMI ARUNACHALAM Sri Nithyananda, born on 13 March 1977, is not subject to an INTERPOL Notice or diffusion.

Done in Lyon, on 22 November 2022.

RETARIAT GENE CMERAL AL SECRETARIAT

Office of Legal Affairs General Secretariat INTERPOL
