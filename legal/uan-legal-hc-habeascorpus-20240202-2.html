UAN-LEGAL-HC-HabeasCorpus-20240202-2

Judgment in Special Criminal Application (Habeas Corpus) No. 9973 of 2019, Janardhana Ramkrishna Sharma v. State of Gujarat

Judgment of the High Court of Gujarat at Ahmedabad in a habeas corpus petition filed by Janardhana Ramkrishna Sharma seeking the production and custody of his two adult daughters, Lopamudra (alias Tatvapriya) and Nandhitha (alias Nityanandita) Janardhana Sharma, who reside outside India at a spiritual organization (Nithyananda Dhyanpeetham). The petition also sought investigation into the welfare of other minors at the organization's affiliated branch in Ahmedabad. The court examined the case through multiple procedural stages including video conferencing with the respondents located in Jamaica.
