Appeal Decision on Passport Impounding Order under Section 11 of the Passports Act, 1967 — Sri Nithyananda Swami

A decision by the Ministry of External Affairs (CPV Division), Government of India, on an appeal filed by Sri Nithyananda Swami against the impounding of his passport by the Regional Passport Office, Bangalore. The appellant's passport had been impounded following notification of criminal cases registered against him. The appeal was heard on 3 August 2010, and the appellant presented judgments from the High Court of Karnataka indicating that investigation was ongoing with no firm evidence presented and that bail had been granted without passport surrender as a condition.
