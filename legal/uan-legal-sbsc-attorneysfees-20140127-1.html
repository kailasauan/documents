Order Granting Defendants' Motion for Attorney's Fees

A judicial order issued by the Superior Court of California, County of San Bernardino, in a civil case between plaintiff Vinay Bharadwaj and defendants including Sri Swami Nithyananda and related foundations. The court addresses a motion for attorney's fees filed by the defendants following dismissal of the underlying action and subsequent appellate proceedings.
