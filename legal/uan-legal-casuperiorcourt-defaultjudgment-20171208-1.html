UAN-LEGAL-CASuperiorCourt-DefaultJudgment-20171208-1

Judgment by Default in Life Bliss Foundation v. Samaya TV et al

A default judgment issued by the Superior Court of California, County of San Bernardino. The plaintiff Life Bliss Foundation, a California non-profit corporation, brought suit against defendants Samaya TV (an Indian company), RSM Broadcasters Private Limited (an Indian company), Ranganath Bharadwaj, and A.V. Kalavathi Bharadwaj. The judgment was entered after defendants failed to answer or appear to defend within the time allowed by law.
