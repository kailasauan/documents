Opinion on Refugee Status of Paramahamsa Nithyananda

A legal opinion by Geoffrey Robertson QC advising on the potential refugee status of Paramahamsa Nithyananda, leader of the Adi Shaivite Minority Tradition. The document examines claims of persecution in India, including mob violence, attacks on temples and properties, false allegations, prolonged criminal proceedings, and media incitement, in relation to refugee protection under the 1951 Geneva Convention.

What this record covers
  • The document examines whether Paramahamsa Nithyananda, a religious leader of the Adi Shaivite Minority Tradition, meets the criteria for refugee status under the 1951 Geneva Convention. The opinion addresses mob violence, temple attacks, and alleged assassination attempts against the subject and his followers in Tamil Nadu and Karnataka from 2010 onwards. The document evaluates a prolonged criminal prosecution spanning nine years in Indian courts and the role of media incitement and political hostility in generating persecution. The opinion assesses whether the subject's fear of persecution is both genuine and well-founded under international refugee law.
