UAN-LEGAL-HC-ForeignJudgmentEnforcement-20190527-1

Judgment in O.S. No. 5239/2017: Nithyananda Dhanapeetam of Columbus v. Smt. Aarathi S. Rao

A judgment of the XLIV Additional City Civil & Sessions Judge, Bengaluru, dated 27 May 2019, in a money suit filed by Nithyananda Dhanapeetam (a non-profit religious corporation registered in Ohio, United States) against the defendant Smt. Aarathi S. Rao, a resident of Bangalore. The suit sought enforcement in India of a foreign judgment obtained by the plaintiff in the District Court of the United States of America (Case No. 2:13 CV 526). The defendant did not appear in the proceedings (ex parte hearing).
