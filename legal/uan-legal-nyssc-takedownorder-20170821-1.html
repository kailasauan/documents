UAN-LEGAL-NYSSC-TakedownOrder-20170821-1

Order on Motion for Preliminary Injunction and Takedown Order, Nithyananda Dhyanapeetam of New York v. Nithyananada Fraud

A judicial order issued by the Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of Bronx, in a case brought by Nithyananda Dhyanapeetam of New York against an anonymous defendant. The order concerns alleged defamatory content on multiple blogs and websites. The court ruled on a motion for preliminary injunction and takedown order after the defendant failed to appear.
