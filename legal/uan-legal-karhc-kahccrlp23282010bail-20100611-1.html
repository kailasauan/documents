6.The offences alleged against the accused No.1 are punishable under Sections 295-A. 376, 377, 420, 506 sub- section (1) & 120-B of IPC. Section 295-A of IPC deals with a person, who with deliberate and malicious intention outraging the religious feelings of any class of citizens of India. by words, either spoken or written, or by signs or by visible representations or otherwise, insults or attempts to insult the religion or the religious beliefs of that class, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to three years, or with fine, or with both.

11.In these circumstances, the question arises as regard to the life and liberty of an accused. Under Article 21 of the Constitution of India. no person would be denied of right to life and personal liberty except in accordance with law. In order to detain or deny the right of liberty, there must be something against the accused to detain him. serious offences are alleged against the accused Ne 1, but same is not supported by any material. Just based on the allegation, the liberty of person cannot be denied. Further, the Investigating Officer has filed a memo stating that there is no material against the accused and has also stated that. presence of the accused is not required for investigation.

12.Hence: in the circumnstances stated above, I find that the accused are required to be enlarged on bail.
