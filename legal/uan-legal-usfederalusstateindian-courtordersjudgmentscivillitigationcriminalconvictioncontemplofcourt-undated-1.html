Compilation of U.S. and Indian Court Documents and Legal Correspondence Regarding Disputes Involving Sri Nithyananda Paramashivam and Related Entities

A compilation, not a primary document

This record was assembled by the Secretariat from other documents and carries its own selection, arrangement and commentary. It is published as a finding aid to the documents it cites, which are primary and are held in this registry under their own references. Where it differs from a document it cites, the document governs.

This document is a compilation of court orders, judgments, judicial correspondence, and legal briefs drawn from U.S. federal and state courts (California, Michigan, Washington, Ohio) and an Indian state court (Karnataka), spanning approximately 2010–2017. It includes civil judgments, criminal convictions, contempt orders, bench warrants, and correspondence from law enforcement and attorneys regarding disputes involving religious figure Sri Nithyananda Paramashivam and various named parties. The compilation presents judicial records and legal analysis concerning multiple litigation matters across multiple jurisdictions.

On July 31, 2012, Mr. Bharadwaj's criminal trial commenced in Washington. Mr. Bharadwaj waived a jury trial.
