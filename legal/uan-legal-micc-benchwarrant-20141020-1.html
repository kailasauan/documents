Bench Warrant for Arrest of Aarthi Sethumadhavan Rao

A bench warrant issued by the Washtenaw County Circuit Court in Michigan in a civil case (Life Bliss Foundation v. Aarthi S. Rao) commanding any peace officer or court officer to arrest the named defendant for failure to appear before the court as ordered. The warrant specifies personal identifying information, sets a cash-performance bond amount, and directs that the defendant be brought before the court immediately.
