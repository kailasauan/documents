The brief facts of the plaintiff's-foundation case is as . under:

The defendant was a volunteer pujari and meditation teacher at the Nithyananda Vedic Templ in seattle from approximately 2007 through 2009. From 2008 through 2009, the defendant misused his position and sexually molested and abused the least one minor child at the Seattle Temple. 3. It is further submitted that the defendant learned that the minor child would not keep secret what he had done her. despite his manipulative attempts to silence her and that the family of the minor child was ready to bring him to justice and the defendant started to lay the groundwork for a defense and developed false accusations against Sri. Therefore in view of the above discussion and reasonings, I am of the opinion that the plaintiff-foundation is entitled for recovery of money with interest from the defendant as claimed in the present suit.

19.So far as the rate of interest is concerned, the plaintiff claimed interest at the rate of 18 % p.a. on the suit claim amount of Rs. 2.74,94,447.50/ -. Therefore this court is of the opinion that the rate of interest claimed by the plaintiff-foundation on the suit claim amount is not reasonable. Hence this court of the opinion that it is just and proper to award interest at the rate of 9% p.a. on the suit claim amount from the defendant.
