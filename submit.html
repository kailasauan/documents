Each record is published on its own page, under a permanent reference number that is also its citation.

The reference number is issued by the Secretariat once the document is registered. It is not chosen by the person submitting.

Submit a document

Use this form to place a document into the Documents Collection. Upload the document itself and record what is written on it. Only the first few entries are required — everything else may be left blank, and the Secretariat will complete it.

Record what the document says, not what you know. Where the document states nothing — no date, no title, no issuing authority — leave the entry empty rather than supplying one. An entry supplied from memory is indistinguishable on the published page from one the document carries.

1 — The document
One document per submission. PDF is preferred. A photographed or scanned document is accepted.
