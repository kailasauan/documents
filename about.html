About this collection

The United Ancient Nations Documents Collection is the public reference copy of the documentary record held by the Secretariat.

What is published here

The collection brings together four bodies of material: the treaties and agreements concluded by the United Ancient Nations and its member nations; reports, submissions and independent expert opinions; judicial and administrative records; and the historical archives of the ancient nations. Each record is published on its own page, in full text. A collection with no records in it yet says so, and shows nothing in their place.

Why the full text

A document that exists only as a scanned file is, for practical purposes, unreadable — by search engines, by assistive technology, and by anyone reading on a small screen. Every record in this collection is therefore transcribed and published as text, on a page of its own, with its reference number, date, author and subjects recorded alongside it.

How a record reaches this collection

A record is digitised, transcribed, described, given a reference number and published. Transcriptions are published as the document was filed, without editorial correction, so that the text on this site can be compared line for line with the original. Where a transcription was produced by optical character recognition, the record page says so.

The reference number

Every record carries a reference number assigned by the Secretariat, which stays with the record for the life of the collection:

ORG - RECORD TYPE - SUBCATEGORY - KEYWORD - YYYYMMDD - VERSION

  • ORG names the publishing body — UAN.
  • RECORD TYPE is the collection: TREATY, REPORT, LEGAL or ARCHIVE.
  • SUBCATEGORY names the forum or authority the record belongs to — UNHRC for the Human Rights Council, UNSR for a Special Rapporteur, GUJHC for the High Court of Gujarat.
  • KEYWORD distinguishes the record from its siblings in the same series.
  • YYYYMMDD is the date of the record. Where the document states only a month or only a year, the unknown part is written as zeros — 20230000 is a record dated 2023 with no month or day stated in the document itself.
  • VERSION rises if a record is ever republished.

The lower-case reference number is also the address of the record page, so the citation and the link are the same string.

Accuracy and corrections

A correction may be sent to the Secretariat at documents@unitedancientnations.org and will be recorded against the record. Certified copies of any record may be requested from the same address.

Citation

Cite a record by its author, title and reference number, followed by the name of this collection. Every record page carries the citation in the form to use, and the reference number is also the address of the page, so a citation can be resolved by reading it.
