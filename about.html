About this collection

The United Ancient Nations Documents Collection is the public reference copy of the documentary record held by the Secretariat.

What is published here

The collection brings together four bodies of material: the treaties and agreements concluded by the United Ancient Nations and its member nations; reports, submissions and independent expert opinions; judicial and administrative records; and the historical archives of the ancient nations. Each record is published on its own page, in full text.

Why the full text

A document that exists only as a scanned file is, for practical purposes, unreadable — by search engines, by assistive technology, and by anyone reading on a small screen. Every record in this collection is therefore transcribed and published as text, on a page of its own, with its reference number, date, parties and subjects recorded alongside it.

How a record reaches this collection

A record is digitised, transcribed, checked against the original, described, and cleared for publication. The reference number assigned by the Secretariat stays with the record for the life of the collection and is the citation to use.

Accuracy and corrections

Transcriptions are checked, and errors are still possible. A correction may be sent to the Secretariat at documents@unitedancientnations.org and will be recorded against the record. Certified copies of any record may be requested from the same address.

Citation

Cite a record by its title and reference number, followed by the name of this collection — for example: Treaty of Friendship, Recognition and Cooperation, UAN/TS/2023/0001, United Ancient Nations Documents Collection.
