Expert Report on Statelessness and the Right to Documentation

Analysis of the legal position of persons deprived of documentation, and of the obligations of receiving states under the applicable conventions.

Full text of the record

1. Introduction. This report has been prepared at the request of the requesting body and sets out the findings of the author following an examination of the documentary record, interviews with affected persons and a review of the applicable legal framework.

2. Methodology. The author has proceeded on the basis of primary documents where these were available, and has indicated in each instance where a finding rests on secondary material.

3. Findings. The findings are set out under headings corresponding to the questions referred to the author, and are supported by the annexes reproduced at the end of this record.

4. Conclusions and recommendations. The conclusions of the author are stated separately from the findings of fact, and the recommendations are addressed to the bodies named in each case.
