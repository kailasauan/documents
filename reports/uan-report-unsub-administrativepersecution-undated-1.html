Human Rights Implications of Administrative Measures to Counter Terrorism and Violent Extremism

This submission by Kailash Union documents alleged human rights violations arising from administrative measures and counter-terrorism actions in India targeting the Supreme Pontiff of Hinduism and KAILASA's communities. The report argues that administrative detention, media-driven persecution, forcible closure of educational institutions, arbitrary arrest, medical torture, and passport revocation constitute violations of international human rights law, including Articles 6, 7, 9, 18, 19 and 26 of the ICCPR and Article 15 of the UDHR.
