KAILASA has established feedback mechanisms that allow children to express their opinions on policies and practices impacting their lives. Additionally, KAILASA Gurukul children are involved in the justice process through local and international youth councils, ensuring their voices are heard in decisions affecting their education and welfare. KAILASA’s policies adhere to comprehensive child protection protocols, digital safety guidelines, and anti-discrimination policies aligned with UNICEF and UNESCO standards. In compliance with the “all knowledge is free” clause of Paramashiva’s economic policy SPH has passed an Executive Order with all knowledge providing services of KAILASA being free of charge.

SPH's global initiatives to ensure children’s rights include:

  • Empowering indigenous children since 1996
  • 5+ million reached worldwide
  • 4 flagship programs: eN-GeniusSM, Nithyananda YogaSM, Nithyananda VidyalayaSM, e-GurukulsSM
  • 50,000+ children empowered annually

To advocate for indigenous educational rights, and enhance children's rights and access to justice, KAILASA collaborates with government agencies, legislative bodies, the United Nations, civil societies, and international organizations.
