The Right to Education, Advances and Challenges: A Report by Kailash Union

This submission to the Special Rapporteur on the right to education addresses advances and challenges in implementing the right to education. The document presents Kailash Union's perspective on education as a fundamental human right, describes the educational institutions and programs operated by the organization including Gurukuls and the Nithyananda Hindu University, and discusses obstacles to indigenous Hindu education systems including alleged persecution and discrimination over several decades.
