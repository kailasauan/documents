This thematic report submitted to the UN Human Rights Council by Kailash Union addresses poverty eradication in a post-growth context and preparation for future development goals. The document argues that KAILASA's governance model incorporates non-GDP indicators of social progress, mechanisms to ensure equitable distribution of benefits, and alternative development pathways prioritizing collective welfare over economic growth.
