Submission on Disinformation and Freedom of Expression During Armed Conflict: Case Study of the Adi Shaivite Velallar Community

This submission addresses the use of disinformation, misinformation and propaganda in armed conflict, responding to General Assembly questions on specific situations, roles of state and non-state actors, and impacts on human rights and vulnerable groups. The submission documents a case study concerning the Adi Shaivite Velallar community, an indigenous religious group within Hinduism, and their leader, describing allegations that media disinformation campaigns have been deployed to sustain persecution, including false criminal cases, sexual assaults, and destruction of property over a ten-year period.
