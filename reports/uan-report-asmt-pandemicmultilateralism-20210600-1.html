UAN-REPORT-ASMT-PandemicMultilateralism-20210600-1

COVID-19 and Multilateralism: A Report by the Adi Shaivite Minority Tradition Hindu Community Kailash Union

A policy report issued by the Adi Shaivite Minority Tradition (ASMT) Hindu Community and Kailash Union in June 2021, addressing global pandemic response and international cooperation. The document identifies obstacles in pandemic management—including vaccine distribution inequities, economic disruption, supply chain fragility, and employment crises—and proposes solutions grounded in Hindu philosophical principles and traditional economic systems. It advocates for revised economic policies prioritizing human welfare, multilateral cooperation based on the concept of Vasudeva Kutumbhakam, community quarantine facilities, public health monitoring, integration of traditional medicine systems, and revival of historical food grain banking models.

What this record covers
