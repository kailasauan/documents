Date: - 19-07-2010 Mr.Vinayak N.Urankar Detective Inspector Cyber Crime Police Station C.I.D, Head Quarters No-1 Carlton House, Palace Road Bangalore-01

Respected Sir, Ref: - Your office letter dated 19-06-2010 with regard to CCPS Cr.No.10/2010 U/s.66, 84(B) of IT Act 2000and Sec.186, 379 r/w.120 (B) IPC With reference to the above letter we would like to state that the programme "RED ALERT" is a private producer programme done by M/s.Sinchana Media. When a programme is done for us by a private producer the original tape after telecast is taken by the concerned producer after a period of one week and we do not have a system of storing the same in our library. We have already called upon the representatives of M/s.Sinchana Media to make available a copy of the same to you and you may kindly collect the same from their end. As far as the agreement is concerned we would like to state that it is a private document between us and M/s.Sinchana Media and as per our policy matters we will not be able to provide you with a copy of the same. We are really sorry for the same and hope you will understand our position on this sir. Thanking You, Yours faithfully, For M/s.Sun TV Network Ltd (Udaya TV)
