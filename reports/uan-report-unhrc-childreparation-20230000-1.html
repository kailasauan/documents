Submission on Reparation for Child Victims and Survivors of Sale and Sexual Exploitation

This submission to the Office of the Special Rapporteur on the sale and sexual exploitation of children addresses reparation needs and mechanisms for child victims and survivors in conflict and non-conflict settings. The document outlines current needs including individual and collective reparations, healthcare, education, skills training, and meaningful victim participation. It identifies gaps in international human rights frameworks, documents specific cases of alleged targeting of members of the Adi Shaivite Minority Tradition (ASMT) community and indigenous spiritual traditions, and proposes measures to improve access to justice and reparation processes.
