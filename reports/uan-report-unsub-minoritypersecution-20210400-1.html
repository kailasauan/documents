UAN-REPORT-UNSUB-MinorityPersecution-20210400-1

A Report by the ASMT Hindu Community

The report documents allegations of human rights violations against the Adi Shaivite Minority Tradition (ASMT) community in India, claiming systematic persecution by what it identifies as neo-Hindutva extremist elements. The submission argues that ASMT women, children, and nuns have been targeted through media misinformation campaigns, deepfake videos, and sexual objectification, and that the Indian legal system has proven ineffective in providing recourse. The report contrasts the lack of enforcement in Indian courts with a US court penalty of USD 5 million against media defendants.
