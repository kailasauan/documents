UAN-REPORT-HumanRights-ReligiousIntolerance-20190428-1

Victimization by video

An essay by David Matas addressing alleged persecution of the Adi Shaivite Minority Tradition and its spiritual leader Sri Nithyananda Paramashivam in India. The document describes attacks by various groups opposed to the Tradition, discusses a fabricated video used in campaigns against the spiritual leader, compares the situation to persecution of Falun Gong in China, and calls on Indian democratic institutions to address incitement to religious intolerance.
