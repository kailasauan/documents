Forensic Video Expert's Preliminary Report on Video Evidence for Life Bliss Foundation

A preliminary forensic video analysis report prepared by Yonovitz & Joe, L.L.P., forensic audio/video experts, commissioned by the Life Bliss Foundation. The report examines a 23-minute silent video recording, six YouTube videos derived from it, and two Forensic Science Laboratory examination reports from the Government of Delhi. The analysis focuses on video authenticity, editing, alterations, and the reliability of underlying forensic examinations.

What this record covers
  • The report analyzes a silent video recording and multiple YouTube versions to assess whether the video is an authentic reproduction of an original recording or has been edited and altered. The report examines six YouTube videos and two Indian government forensic examination reports, documenting extensive editing, insertions of audio content, and other modifications in the public versions. The analysis identifies deficiencies in the Indian Forensic Science Laboratory reports, including lack of detail, inconsistencies, and insufficient documentation of methodology. The report concludes the 23-minute video at issue is an edited version of an original recording and that the forensic examination reports lack sufficient detail to authenticate the source material.
