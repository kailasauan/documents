UAN-REPORT-HRL-ReligiousIntolerance-20190428-1

Opinion on Human Rights Violations Against Paramahamsa Nithyananda and the Adi Shaivite Minority Tradition

A legal opinion and accompanying article by international human rights lawyer David Matas concerning alleged human rights violations perpetrated against Paramahamsa Nithyananda (His Divine Holiness Sri Nithyananda Paramashivam) and the Adi Shaivite Minority Tradition in India. The document describes claims of religious intolerance, violent attacks, official harassment, and the circulation of a fabricated video, and concludes there is a prima facie case of human rights violations. Matas indicates his intention to conduct further investigations and prepare a formal report.

What this record covers
  • The document presents a human rights lawyer's preliminary assessment of alleged persecution of a Hindu minority tradition and its spiritual leader based on religious intolerance in India; The document discusses the role of fabricated video evidence in campaigns against the tradition and draws parallels to similar tactics used against Falun Gong in China; The document examines state capacity and willingness to protect religious minorities and contrasts India's democratic institutions with authoritarian systems.
