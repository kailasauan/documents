A report by Kailash Union

Hindu communities face acute vulnerabilities within the landscape of the global displacement crisis.

The lack of formal recognition of Hinduism as a religion in over 66 countries underscores systemic barriers to religious freedom faced by displaced Hindu communities globally.¹

27 countries officially endorse Islam as a state religion, while 13 recognize Christianity, but none accord Hinduism similar status².
