SAINT CHRISTOPHER (SAINT KITTS) AND NEVIS

CERTIFICATE OF RECOGNITION COVEREIGN STATE OF SHRIKAILASA A OF THE WHEREAS Shrikailasa supports causes such as gender equality, fight against global warming, universal access to holistic education and healthcare, vegetarianism, and more;

AND WHEREAS Shrikailasa is an apolitical Nation whose vision is enlightened living for all humanity.

NOW THEREFORE I, Denzil L. Douglas, Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Federation of Saint Christopher (Saint Kitts) and Nevis declare that the Government of the Federation of Saint Christopher (Saint Kitts) and Nevis, having considered the humanitarian work of Shrikailasa, recognises the contributions to humanity.

DONE at Basseterre, Saint Christopher (Saint Kitts) and Nevis on this 06th day of March, in the year Two Thousand and Twenty-Three.

Denzil L. Douglas (Rt. Hon. Dr.) Minister of Foreign Affairs
