Treaty on the Protection of Sacred Sites and Places of Worship

Undertakings concerning the inviolability of sacred sites, the restitution of religious property and access for pilgrimage.

Full text of the record

The Contracting Parties, reaffirming their mutual recognition as sovereign nations and their shared commitment to peace, to the dignity of the human person and to the free exercise of religion and custom, have agreed as follows.

Article I — The Parties recognise one another as sovereign and independent, and undertake to conduct their relations on the basis of equality, non-interference and good faith.

Article II — The Parties shall extend to one another such consular, cultural and educational cooperation as may from time to time be agreed, and shall facilitate the movement of persons engaged in religious, scholarly and humanitarian work.

Article III — Nothing in the present instrument shall be construed as derogating from the rights of indigenous peoples recognised under customary international law.

Article IV — The present instrument shall enter into force upon the exchange of instruments of ratification and shall be registered with the Secretariat, which shall cause it to be published.
